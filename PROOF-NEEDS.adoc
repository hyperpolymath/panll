= Proof needs — PanLL
:toc: macro

What PanLL proves today, what it deliberately does not, and what is still
owed. Escape hatches and their dispositions are tracked separately in
link:docs/proof-debt.adoc[`docs/proof-debt.adoc`].

Last reconciled against the tree: 2026-10-26 (`main` at `1937c6b`).

toc::[]

== Current state

The only machine-checked proofs live in the panel-clades ABI,
`panel-clades/src/abi/PanelClades/ABI/` (Idris2 0.8.0, `%default total`,
about 1,300 lines). As of the date above, no `.idr` file contains a hole,
`believe_me`, `assert_total`, `assert_smaller`, `idris_crash`,
`unsafePerformIO` or `postulate`.

The CI job `panel-clades-abi` in `.github/workflows/build-validation.yml`
runs `panel-clades/scripts/check-abi.sh`, which:

* builds typed-wasm's grade kernel at the pin in
  `panel-clades/scripts/fetch-siblings.sh` (`117bbf8`);
* builds `panel-clades/src/abi/panel-clades-grades.ipkg` against it and
  fails on any `Error:` line, even when `idris2` exits 0;
* fails if any module under `PanelClades/` is missing from the ipkg, or
  contains `believe_me`, `assert_total`, `assert_smaller`, `idris_crash`,
  `unsafePerformIO` or `postulate` (a keyword scan; it does not look for
  holes);
* fails if `Budgets.idr` is stale against the clade configs
  (`scripts/gen-budgets.sh --check`);
* requires each of the six fixtures in `panel-clades/tests/reject/` to be
  refused at its `invalid` declaration, with the expected message.

=== What is proved

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Module |Proved

|`Types.idr`
|The C tag encodings of `Result` and `CladeKind` round-trip, so they are
injective (`resultRoundTrip`, `cladeKindRoundTrip`). Equality on both is
decidable without a catch-all `absurd` (`decEqViaTag`). `allCladeKinds`
lists every kind (`cladeKindExhaustive`) and has length `cladeKindCount`.
A `Handle` is never null and a `CladeId` is never empty, by construction.

|`Layout.idr`
|Rounding up to an alignment lands on a multiple of it (`alignUpCorrect`).
Every computed struct size is a multiple of its alignment
(`calcStructSizeAligned`). Field alignment is decided by an equation on
`Nat` (`decDivides`, `proveFieldsAligned`), not by a Boolean test.

|`Grades.idr`
|Clade resource contracts are graded in typed-wasm's kernel (transcribed
from hyperpolymath/resource-algebra), not re-declared. Occupancy composes
within a bound (`phasesWithin`). Loss adds across two crossings
(`crossTwice`), and the better of two alternatives stays within a shared
bound (`betterOfTwo`). Occupancy and cost are kept apart
(`panelPhasesOccupancy` vs `panelPhasesCost`). The neurosym-bridge
contract typechecks against its declared budget
(`neurosymBridgeContract`).

|`Budgets.idr`
|Generated from the clades' `config.k9.ncl` resource blocks. It proves
nothing itself; it is the input `Grades.idr` checks against. Only
neurosym-bridge declares a budget so far.

|`Foreign.idr`
|Typechecks only. It declares the Zig FFI surface; it proves nothing.
|===

The reject fixtures pin down what the types refuse: `BudgetTooSmall`,
`CollapseWithinTolerance`, `MisalignedField`, `NullHandle`, `OverBudget`
and `WasmPointerIsEightBytes`.

=== What is not proved

These limits are stated in the modules themselves.

* *Struct size covers the last field.* `Layout.idr` does not prove that a
  computed size is at least the end of the last field; `verifyLayout`
  checks it at run time and refuses a layout that fails.
* *The Zig side matches.* Nothing checks that `panel-clades/ffi/zig/`
  exports the symbols `Foreign.idr` declares, with those types, or uses
  the sizes and tags `Types.idr` proves. `ffi/zig/src/main.zig` restates
  them by hand.
* *Grades reach the runtime.* The budgets are DESIGNED values from the
  configs, not measurements, and nothing in the panel runtime emits or
  checks these grades yet.

== Recommended prover

*Idris2 0.8.0* for anything at the ABI or grade level, matching the
existing modules and the typed-wasm kernel they import.

First-party Rust follows standards `3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` §1.2
(owner ruling 2026-11-07): *Creusot* with *Pearlite* contracts, plus *Verus*.
Tests and the bounded TLA+ model do not discharge implementation obligations.
Pearlite is Creusot's specification language, not an independent prover.

[cols="2,2,3,3",options="header"]
|===
|Implementation entrypoint |Verifier and status |Planned obligation |Trust boundary / next action
|`crates/provenance/src/lib.rs`: `Registry::new`, `verify_chain`
|Creusot / Pearlite: OPEN
|Registry validity, chain predecessor handling and propagation of invalid or
claimed ancestry to later verdicts.
|Write contracts on the shipped Rust and qualify supported collection/string
operations. No implementation proof has been run for these entrypoints.
|Action producers and provenance-recording call sites
|Creusot / Pearlite and Verus: OPEN (specification prerequisite)
|Define which actions must produce records and establish producer-side coverage,
including failure and cancellation paths. Verifying supplied records does not
prove that every required action was recorded.
|Inventory the producers and specify the recording obligation before assigning
implementation contracts; no completeness proof is claimed.
|`crates/provenance/src/lib.rs`: `verify_entry`, `verify_witnesses`
|Verus: OPEN
|A verified verdict requires the supplied bytes to match the signed content
hash, valid author binding and every declared independent witness; rejected or
missing evidence cannot promote a verdict.
|Specify the production status transitions and establish a checked connection
to the shipped functions. No separate model counts as a proof of them.
|`canonical`, `hash`, `sign`, `attest`, signature verification and timestamp parsing
|First-party wrappers: Creusot / Pearlite and Verus OPEN
|Prove the wrappers preserve their input/output and error contracts, including
canonicalisation of `content_hash` and the fields bound by signatures and
attestations. Allocate the obligations against the shipped functions.
|Only third-party JCS, SHA3-256, ML-DSA-65, entropy and time-parsing primitives
and their specified behaviour are ASSUMED. Record exact locked versions and
extern contracts when proofs are introduced; cryptographic hardness, civil
identity approval and independent witness judgment are outside these proofs.
|`src-gossamer/src/ipc.rs` dispatch and `tools/pcc` propagation
|Creusot / Pearlite and Verus: OPEN
|Allocate implementation contracts after writing the dispatch/propagation
specifications; retain current tests as TESTED evidence only.
|PCC frontend scanning remains blocked on the AffineScript port. Neither
verifier is silently excluded because that separate dependency is unfinished.
|===

Any future proof receipt must name source/tool/solver versions, invocation,
discharged obligations and assumptions. Shared SMT solvers or specifications
do not establish independent witnesses. Approved contributor keys, independent
witnesses and trusted checkpoints remain separate operational requirements.

== Priority

. Struct size lower bound and the safe callback wrapper (#149): small,
  local, and they close the two gaps the modules admit to.
. Zig/Idris FFI agreement check: cheap, and it removes the largest
  unchecked assumption under the proofs.
. PCC soundness: the highest-value proof, but only after the AffineScript
  port (#148) gives it something to check.
