= Proof needs — PanLL
:toc: macro

What PanLL proves today, what it deliberately does not, and what is still
owed. Escape hatches and their dispositions are tracked separately in
link:docs/proof-debt.adoc[`docs/proof-debt.adoc`].

This file follows the estate proof policy,
https://github.com/metadatastician/standards/blob/9bdf864cc7f2c8e24b4df84bf00d04158eee3ad5/3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc[standards `3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc`]
(read at `9bdf864`). Every row carries one of its closed status tokens (§5):
`PROVEN`, `PROVEN-WITH-AXIOMS`, `TESTED` (with bounds), `ASSUMED` or `OPEN`.

*Proof Witness Grades do not apply.* Every PanLL obligation is engineering:
each names a PanLL type, function, ABI or wire format (PROOF-POLICY §1). PanLL
has no theory obligations of its own, so no row has a PWG (§8.1). The
max-plus/min-plus kernel lemmas it imports are theory owned by
hyperpolymath/resource-algebra; their grade is recorded there.

Last reconciled against the tree: 2026-11-09 (`main` at `7d1feaa`, plus the
change that added this line).

== Current state

The only kernel-checked proofs live in the panel-clades ABI,
`panel-clades/src/abi/PanelClades/ABI/` (Idris2 0.8.0, `%default total`,
about 1,300 lines).

The CI job `panel-clades-abi` in `.github/workflows/build-validation.yml`
runs `panel-clades/scripts/check-abi.sh`, which:

* builds typed-wasm's grade kernel at the pin in
  `panel-clades/scripts/fetch-siblings.sh` (`117bbf8`);
* builds `panel-clades/src/abi/panel-clades-grades.ipkg` against it and
  fails on any `Error:` line, even when `idris2` exits 0;
* fails if any module under `PanelClades/` is missing from the ipkg;
* runs `panel-clades/scripts/scan-escape-hatches.sh` over the whole import
  cone (the five PanelClades modules and every typed-wasm kernel module at
  the pin). It fails on `believe_me`, `assert_total`, `assert_smaller`,
  `idris_crash`, `unsafePerformIO` or `postulate`, on a hole (`?name`:
  `idris2 --build` accepts unsolved holes), on `partial` or `covering`, and
  on a module without `%default total`. Comments are ignored;
* fails if `Budgets.idr` is stale against the clade configs
  (`scripts/gen-budgets.sh --check`);
* requires each of the six fixtures in `panel-clades/tests/reject/` to be
  refused at its `invalid` declaration, with the expected message.

=== What is proved

Receipt: Build Validation run
https://github.com/hyperpolymath/panll/actions/runs/42408104327[42408104327],
job `panel-clades-abi`, on `main` at
`7d1feaaf112b3d91fd504aaf871be5ae49840434`, 2026-11-07: the package built
with 0 errors and all six fixtures were refused for the expected reasons.
The import-cone scan was added after that run; its first receipt is the CI
run of the change that added it.

[cols="1,3,1",options="header"]
|===
|Module |Proved |Status

|`Types.idr`
|The C tag encodings of `Result` and `CladeKind` round-trip, so they are
injective (`resultRoundTrip`, `cladeKindRoundTrip`). Equality on both is
decidable without a catch-all `absurd` (`decEqViaTag`). `allCladeKinds`
lists every kind (`cladeKindExhaustive`) and has length `cladeKindCount`.
A `Handle` is never null and a `CladeId` is never empty, by construction.
|`PROVEN`

|`Layout.idr`
|Rounding up to an alignment lands on a multiple of it (`alignUpCorrect`).
Every computed struct size is a multiple of its alignment
(`calcStructSizeAligned`). `decDivides` returns a `Divides` witness when an
offset is a multiple of its alignment, and `proveFieldsAligned` builds the
`FieldsAligned` proof from those witnesses. `decDivides` is a semi-decision:
`Nothing` carries no proof that the field is misaligned.
|`PROVEN`

|`Grades.idr`
|Clade resource contracts are graded in typed-wasm's kernel, not
re-declared. Occupancy composes within a bound (`phasesWithin`). Loss adds
across two crossings (`crossTwice`), and the better of two alternatives
stays within a shared bound (`betterOfTwo`). Occupancy and cost are kept
apart (`panelPhasesOccupancy` vs `panelPhasesCost`). The neurosym-bridge
contract typechecks against its declared budget (`neurosymBridgeContract`).
These hold of the kernel as transcribed in typed-wasm; that the
transcription matches resource-algebra is the separate *Grade kernel
identity* row.
|`PROVEN`

|`Budgets.idr`
|Generated from the clades' `config.k9.ncl` resource blocks. It proves
nothing itself; it is the input `Grades.idr` checks against. Only
neurosym-bridge declares a budget so far. The budgets are DESIGNED values,
not measurements.
|— (input)

|`Foreign.idr`
|Typechecks only. It declares the Zig FFI surface with `%foreign`; the C
side is not checked against it.
|`ASSUMED`
|===

The reject fixtures pin down what the types refuse: `BudgetTooSmall`,
`CollapseWithinTolerance`, `MisalignedField`, `NullHandle`, `OverBudget`
and `WasmPointerIsEightBytes`.

=== What is not proved

These limits are stated in the modules themselves.

* *Struct size covers the last field.* `Layout.idr` does not prove that a
  computed size is at least the end of the last field; `verifyLayout`
  checks it at run time and refuses a layout that fails.
* *The Zig side matches.* Nothing checks that `panel-clades/ffi/zig/`
  exports the symbols `Foreign.idr` declares, with those types, or uses
  the sizes and tags `Types.idr` proves. `ffi/zig/src/main.zig` restates
  them by hand.
* *Grades reach the runtime.* The budgets are DESIGNED values from the
  configs, not measurements, and nothing in the panel runtime emits or
  checks these grades yet.

== Exceptions (PROOF-POLICY §4)

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Field |Record

|Artefact
|`docs/models/Provenance.tla` with `Provenance.cfg`,
`ProvenanceNoWitness.cfg` and `ProvenanceReachable.cfg` (the record is
repeated in the module header)

|Obligation
|An attacker who can hand the verifier any chain with any content, attach
any published signature, and sign with the author's key once it leaks,
cannot obtain a `verified` verdict for a record the author did not intend,
for content that does not match, or for a broken chain.

|Tool
|TLA+, checked by TLC

|Why not the mapped verifiers
|The property is about an adversary interleaving with honest parties.
Creusot/Pearlite and Verus contracts on `verify_chain` state what one call
returns, not what an attacker can reach. Those implementation obligations
remain `OPEN` below.

|Status
|`TESTED` within the bounds in `Provenance.cfg`: 2 digests, 6 records
(2 roots, 4 children), chains of length 1 or 2, one author and one witness
key. Checked both ways: with the witness requirement removed
(`ProvenanceNoWitness.cfg`) TLC must find a forgery, and
`ProvenanceReachable.cfg` must reach a verified state, so the invariant is
neither vacuous nor true by definition.

|Trust boundary
|Signatures and hashes are perfectly binding (symbolic); the author and
witness keys are not both compromised. Not a cryptographic proof and not an
implementation-correspondence proof.

|Receipt
|CI job `provenance-model` in `.github/workflows/build-validation.yml` runs
`docs/models/check-models.sh` (TLC 2.19, jar SHA-256 pinned) on every push
and pull request; its first run is on the change that rewrote the model. It
was rewritten on 2026-11-09 because the earlier model let any party sign
any payload, so its `Binding` invariant held by definition. The receipt in
`docs/PROVENANCE-EXHIBIT-B.adoc` (TLC 2.19, 2026-11-06) is for that earlier
model, `state: superseded`.

== Recommended prover

*Idris2 0.8.0* for anything at the ABI or grade level, matching the
existing modules and the typed-wasm kernel they import.

First-party Rust follows standards `3-practice/PROOF-POLICY.adoc` §1.2
(owner ruling 2026-11-07): *Creusot* with *Pearlite* contracts, plus *Verus*.
Tests and the bounded TLA+ model do not discharge implementation obligations.
Pearlite is Creusot's specification language, not an independent prover.

Any future proof receipt must name source/tool/solver versions, invocation,
discharged obligations and assumptions. Shared SMT solvers or specifications
do not establish independent witnesses. Approved contributor keys, independent
witnesses and trusted checkpoints remain separate operational requirements.

== Priority

. Struct size lower bound and the safe callback wrapper (#149): small,
  local, and they close the two gaps the modules admit to.
. Zig/Idris FFI agreement check: cheap, and it removes the largest
  unchecked assumption under the proofs.
. PCC soundness: the highest-value proof, but only after the AffineScript
  port (#148) gives it something to check.
